## Scene

**Reflector.** I have read the original. Its research question — *can a critical
approach to the data visualization process produce a design that is able to
undermine simplification of complex data?* — and its brief — *design a
speculative visualization system able to expand beyond machine interpretation
of human emotions* [ORIGINAL: Premise, Research question; Proposition, brief].
The current piece has no decoder-mapper-interpreter loop yet; it has an
on-device embedder, a sensory head, a denoiser-decoder, and a drift knob. So
before aesthetics I want the question of *who the crowd is*. The original's
Mapper was the load-bearing part: *"the designer shares some of their agency
with the crowd by relinquishing part of control but remains in control of the
visualization structure"* [ORIGINAL: Mapper]. In the lab I see no crowd. I see
a knob labelled **drift** and eight seed sentences written by the designer.

**Concept.** Correct that the crowd is absent — and note where it went. The
drift spindle is not the Mapper; it is the *regulated edge* from C2, and it is
doing the Mapper's work early. At Phase D the consensus loop returns and the
crowd becomes the thing that *replaces imported authority*: the Mapper's
temperature. So I read the drift knob as a **staged placeholder** — a
microcosm of the consensus axis. It is honest provided it is declared as such
and yields to the crowd.

## Round I — the drift knob and the architect's hand

**Reflector.** Verdict on the knob: **divergent, and I want it owned.** The
original never had a drift knob, because the variance it generates was meant to
come from *people* — the "living, constantly rebuilding mapping" [ORIGINAL:
Beyond shape; Mapper]. A knob is all agency in the designer's hand. If the
crowd never arrives, this divergence becomes a permanent substitution — the
machine's edge will be *designer's hallucination*, not *collective
negotiation*. Divergence is legal if named. So: name it. The lab's edge is
currently "the designer's idea of the edge."

**Concept.** Agreed, with a sharper cut. The Rel design never trusted its own
imported authority either: *"the data itself is always meta… an unreliable
mediator"* [ORIGINAL: Context, State of the surroundings]. Our REDESIGN
critiqued the original's 9-box as an *imported taxonomy* — but the original's
own text already suspected its black-box quantifiers. The one difference: the
original *showed* the suspicion as its subject matter; our current system needs
to show it too. Drift is **ND** for now — the edge is the designer's — and must
carry a visible admission until the Mapper lands.

**Reflector.** Accepted. Condition recorded: ND with declared provenance.

## Round II — distance between shapes

**Reflector.** The user asked why parts overlap; whether two readings that
differ in content should not overlap. Here the original has already spoken, and
the development is only now rediscovering it. The Constructor principle:
*"A separation into modeling separate shapes, not an entire structure… each
piece of data can be mapped into a separate shape, while the entirety will
still hold all complexity by combining these shapes into a whole"* [ORIGINAL:
Constructor, Visual exploration]; and the placing rule: *"placing emotionally
similar objects closer or even overlapping each other"* [ORIGINAL: Constructor,
Prototype]. Distance between shapes is not decoration — in the original it is
**the mapped meaning**: emotional similarity is *physical* proximity. The
current lab's separation (dominant holds the centre; parts orbit along their
learned directions) therefore **mutates** the original: it revives proximity as
a live variable but derives the direction from the reading, not from a crowd.

**Concept.** Mutation — with one escalation: the original's proximity was
*consensus-steered* (two sentences that differ in content → farther apart,
*because the crowd placed them so*). Our current separation is *reading-
steered* (distance follows the decoded offsets and we stopped equalizing
frame size). Those are related but the semantics differ: consensus-distance is
*about other readings*; reading-distance is *about the parts of one reading*.
Both are valuable. They must not be conflated in the UI — the lab currently
shows one, and the strip/gallery (FR-9/G5, Phase E) will carry the other.

**Reflector.** I accept that distinction. Verdict: **mutates**, with the
proximity-semantics made explicit when the corpus grows.

## Round III — crystalline seams versus the marshmallow

**Reflector.** The collision register shrinks blend radius so the invented
solids meet at visible seams. The original's gesture grammar was crystalline —
*regular forms growing into and out of other such regular forms* [ORIGINAL:
Constructor, Visual exploration, citing the crystal literature]. The
marshmallow the user keeps rejecting (everything welded into one soft lump) is
closer to the *average of the shapes* — precisely what the original's design
*refused*: the mean of {box, cone, cylinder} is not a shape anyone can
inhabit. Collision's seams **resonate**: they are the crystal plane made
visible, the arbitrariness of the sign showing as structure rather than hiding
as finish.

**Concept.** Resonant — provide collision's seams are kept as *honest tension*,
never merchandised into prettiness. The register stays light/happy/colourful
(C7), a pastel field not a filter. Glossy apexes and teetering poses are fine
if they come from the reading's own decoded values (our retune layer derives
everything from decoded params — no gesture libraries). The instant the seam
becomes a *style* rather than a *structure*, the Curator objects.

## Round IV — long sentences collapse

**Reflector.** The lab's measurement: a 26-word, two-clause sentence decodes to
**one** active part, where a 5-word seed yields two. The embedder mean-pools;
structure flattens; the decoder answers with a single louder part. The original
had the opposite motion: shape count and size followed the data — per-sentence
shapes, size modulated by relative word count [ORIGINAL: Constructor]. The
original would call this collapse **divergent** and a symptom, not a feature:
the piece is supposed to *hold complexity by combining separate shapes into a
whole* [ORIGINAL: Constructor], and the current arithmetic folds complexity
away exactly when there is more of it.

**Concept.** Then "richness follows structure" is the original's unclaimed
implication — **mutates**, not invented. Token/clause structure is computable
entirely on-device (privacy intact, QR-3). Whether length *spreads* the parts
or *deepens* one part is a register decision we can now test in the lab and
bring to the D-B review. But it must be a visible convention, shown as such —
"richness follows structure — the machine's grammar" — not an unspoken thumb
on the scales.

## Round V — the register verdict

**Reflector.** The studio's eye has spoken: collision, more drift, "tension
worth staring at." Against the original: *"eliminating readability it has an
opportunity to place importance on conveying understanding rather than data
itself"* [ORIGINAL: Context, State of the practice — the sublime]; *"truth in
meaning over truth in data"*. An edge that is *not* immediately readable is the
original's declared position. **Resonant.** The only caution carried over from
Rounds I–III: the tension must be the *structure's* (seams of separate signs,
real distance, collapse made visible) and not *caption* (a label saying
"this is tension").

**Concept.** Endorsement, same condition. The D-B verdict overall:

| Development choice | Verdict | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Drift spindle (current, pre-Mapper) | **divergent — owned** | Named as designer's-edge until the crowd arrives (Phase D); visible provenance |
| Distance-between-shapes (separation) | **mutates** | Distance semantics explicit: reading-distance now, consensus-distance in the strip/gallery later |
| Crystalline seams (collision register) | **resonant** | Honest tension, never style; derived-only parameter moves |
| Long-sentence collapse | **divergent — to fix or own** | Either "one insistence" (owned divergence) or structure-driven spacing (mutation) — user decides at review |
| High-drift leaning, tension worth staring at | **resonant** | Register stays pastel-biased, light/happy/colourful (C7) |

## Addendum — the long-sentence question, answered by measurement (2026-08-30)

The user chose **richness follows structure**. The lab then measured what that
choice must overcome: a 26-word, two-clause reading decodes to a **literal
one-hot** (voices read `1.00`, tail below ~1e-8). The aesthetic retune layer
flattens weights (`p = 0.35..0.55` at richness 1), which surfaces the
secondary voices of readings that have *small-but-real* tails — but the
one-hot collapse has nothing to surface, and no finite flattening can invent
voices the decoder never made (`w^p` of ~1e-8 stays hidden).

**Conclusion (both agents):** spacing-by-structure works in the aesthetic
layer (already live in the lab); **voice-surfacing is a model-level property**
— structure must enter the *conditioning* (denoiser/decoder training signal
and structure-varied seed corpus), which is a Phase-C training change, not a
retune. The lab states the dead-end plainly in its caption rather than faking
a rich form from default parts (Concept guardrail: never fake an experience
the machine didn't have). Decision recorded: enrich the generator
conditioning with structure in Phase C; the alternative ("one insistence") was
explicitly declined by the user.

### Slice-2 outcome (2026-08-30)
Shipped (commit `c431760`): 401-d conditioning with a dedicated richness
channel into the denoiser body; a 19-seed structure-varied corpus whose rich
readings carry multi-anchor centers; manifest 0.2.0. The decisive discovery on
the way: the **DDIM latent-manifold clamp**. At t→T, ᾱ→0 amplified tiny ε
errors into |z| of *hundreds* — far outside the decoder's trained manifold —
so every live reading decoded to one hot (the underlying "marshmallow/
repetitive" since Phase B, confirmed in the lab). Clamping predX0 to the
trained |z|≲2 space (deterministic, mirrored in `scripts/verify_voices.py`)
latches trajectories on-manifold: every reading now surfaces 4–5 live voices,
and richness-follows-structure holds end to end.

## Round VI — the palette, measured (2026-08-31)

**Scene.** The human called the palette the most affecting choice; the next
step opens with the agent evaluation against the concept (vivid vs pastel,
arbitrariness of hue, per-part spread). The evaluation is grounded in **real
decoded data**, not screenshots: all 19 seeds' `sdfParams.material` from
`public/seed-forms.json`.

**Measured (19 seeds).**

| Axis | Actual | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| Saturation | 0.448–0.524 → **spread 0.076** | a *uniform*, not a field — "rain" and "grey ferry fog" wear the same coat |
| Lightness | 0.658–0.761 (spread 0.103) | narrow, but **structured**: richness 0 → ~0.75, richness 1.0 → ~0.66 — long readings drift deeper |
| Hue | 0.092–0.924 (spread **0.832**) | the only axis with real per-reading variance |
| Per-part spread | hue delta up to **1.54 (full circle)**; sat +0.10→+0.36; light ±0.14 | parts can be any colour, unrelated to their reading's hue |

**Reflector.** The flat decoded coat is the *mean of the shapes* — in colour.
The original refused the average; its Constructor made each data point's part
carry its own character. A palette that decodes to one saturation for every
reading, from "rain" to "wasp-jam-summer", repeats the averaged-to-safety the
piece is supposed to undermine. "Vivid at the edge, quiet at the centre" is a
**mutation** of the original's register — it has no drift axis there — so it
must be owned as such, not smuggled. Endorsement of the pastel *field* (QR-4 /
C7): bias, never clamp — that part is right.

**Concept.** The pastel field holds; what fails is variance. And there is a
seam to name: the vividness the lab currently shows is **register
engineering, not the machine's reading**. At the default collision drift (0.55)
saturation multiplies ×~1.5 and the per-part layer pushes parts to 0.86–1.0 —
while the raw decoded palette sits at ≈0.50 for all 19 seeds. The viewer sees
the interpreter's grammar, louder than the machine's own voice. On hue:
arbitrariness is correct and wide (0.83), but (a) the per-part formula wraps a
full circle — the part→reading colour relationship is *undiscoverable* — and
(b) nothing in the UI presents hue-as-convention. Arbitrariness must be
**shown** (C1; the original's "a reduction of a reduction"), or it reads as
meaning.

**Verdicts.**

| Axis | Verdict | Condition / amendment |
|---|---|---|
| Vivid vs pastel | **amend** | Pastel field stays; saturation needs *decoded edge* — a model-level property (the retune can't invent variance the decoder never made). Ride the item-1 decoder retrain: targets that *use* the band, not centre it. |
| Arbitrariness of hue | **amend** | Cap the per-part hue delta (keep the reading's hue as visible centre of its parts) OR document the full-wrap rule where viewers can find it. |
| Per-part spread | **resonant** (cut) | Cut mode's one-hot fabrics keep edges; soft mode RGB-merges toward muddle — watch when part count rises. |

**Handing back.** This is a first agent pass; the amendments enter the
consensus loop, bound into the item-1 slice (`0.2.1`: blend-as-decoded +
sat-variance targets, same retrain — denoiser untouched). The human decides at
review whether "vivid vs pastel" becomes a *recorded variable* of the reading
or stays a register property. Status: **open — one human decision pending**.

### Round VI closed — human ruling & the 0.2.1 result (2026-08-31)

**Human decision: PASTEL.** Saturation does NOT become a decoded vivid
variable — it stays a register property. The Round VI critique ("a uniform,
not a field") is answered by *variance inside the pastel field*, not by
vividness: each anchor carries its own saturation target inside the [0.1, 0.7]
bias band (raw targets chosen so soft_bias lands ≈ 0.22–0.70), and blends
interpolate material — so richness and anchor mix spread saturation per
reading while the register stays pastel.

**Measured on the regenerated 19-seed corpus (decoder v2, denoiser untouched):**
- saturation spread **0.143–0.710 (spread 0.567)** — the 0.076 uniform is
  dead, nothing leaves the pastel field (0.71 sits inside the soft shoulder);
- blend modes **14 cut / 5 soft** — both grammars present in the automatic
  experience (the convention: solid alphabet cuts, roundedBox/torus/blob morph);
- blob ripple tamed at the target level — decoded displacement max 0.113
  (0.2.0-era ripples ran to the 0.5 clamp);
- voice-surfacing intact — rich readings still decode 4–5 active voices
  (`scripts/verify_voices.py`: fog 5, summer kitchen 3, ferry 4);
- denoiser sha unchanged from 0.2.0; manifest `0.2.1-generated` (slice 3).

**Status: CLOSED — decision recorded, variance shipped as instruction.**

### Round VII — the visitor loop, made legible (2026-09-01)

**Problem (human-raised):** what the visitor is *doing* is never clear. Input
and output are unlabelled, "the crowd" is named three times but never defined,
and the contribution act reads as a consent wall ("published openly under
CC BY-SA") rather than a meaningful act. The loop that is the point of the
piece (words → machine reading → visitor's judgment → the crowd learns) is
invisible.

**Reflector (academic) — verdict: `resonant` with a hard condition; `mutates`
for the crowd model.** The original is a *post-optimal object* and
*"affective over explanatory"* — it chose *user-unfriendliness to provoke*,
*designed ambiguity of the relation between the represented and its
representation*, and *"eliminating readability… truth in meaning over truth in
data."* So clarity is a real risk. But the original never hid the *machinery* —
the machine *"does not take the accuracy of quantification as a given and
admits to possible shortcomings,"* and shows *"a reduction of a reduction."*
Clarifying *who reads and who judges* is showing the machinery honestly:
**resonant.** What the original keeps deliberately ambiguous is **meaning**
(the relation between text and form) — **the condition:** clarify the
mechanism, never the meaning. The crowd model is a **`mutates`**: the original's
Mapper crowd *placed* ("placing emotionally similar objects closer"); ours
*reads* (gives readings) and the mapping is derived later (the aligner, D4).
Owned, not smuggled.

**Concept (Curator) — `approve`, amended.** (1) Arbitrariness visible via "the
crowd is everyone's readings" + "not a description of your words." (2) Meaning
*occasioned* by the visitor's judgment, never claimed by the form. (3) Neutral
on form variance. (4) Register: plain, warm; strip the project's internal jargon
(convention-crystallizer, living distribution) from the UI. (5) Structure not
adjectives. Amendments: define the crowd **once**, keep it short and light
(affective over explanatory), never imply the form describes the words.

**Shipped:** crowd defined once in the subtitle; drift knob = "how close to the
crowd's way of reading?" with "0 = the machine's edge · 1 = the crowd's centre";
output labelled "the machine's reading of your sentence:"; the co-creation panel
opens "your hand — does this reading land?"; contribution reframed as a teaching
gift — "give this reading to the crowd" with *"this is how the machine learns:
give a reading and it joins the crowd. the forms are the machine's way of
reading, not a description of your words. the crowd is still forming — you could
be among the first it learns from."* Gate green (107 unit · lint · typecheck ·
build · e2e chromium).
